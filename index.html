📖 Official Hockey Darts Rules & Guide

1. Offensive Attack & Shots

The team with puck possession shoots first each round by throwing 3 darts to establish their offensive target score. Every completed offensive possession is recorded as a Shot on Goal.

2. Defensive Counter & Goals

The opposing team then takes a counter-throw.
• If the defense's counter score is lower than the offense's target, a Goal is scored.
• If the defense's counter score equals or beats the target (without doubling it), they successfully block the shot—preventing a goal while keeping the puck.

3. Puck Steals

If the defending team's counter score is double or more than the offensive team's target score ($\text{totalScore} \ge \text{offScore} \times 2$), they execute a **Steal**, taking puck possession for the next turn.

4. Assists Calculations

When a goal is scored, assists are automatically evaluated based on progressive scoring progression across the three darts:
• +1 Assist if Dart 3 > Dart 2
• +1 Assist if Dart 2 > Dart 1

5. Penalties & Penalty Box

If a player throws a miss (`0`) on Dart 3 during an offensive turn, their team is assessed a penalty. One player is sent to the Penalty Box for 2 minutes, restricting that team to throwing only 2 darts on their next offensive turn.

6. Game Duration & Overtime

The game consists of 3 regulation periods. If scores are tied at the end of regulation, extra Overtime periods are played until a winner is decided.
